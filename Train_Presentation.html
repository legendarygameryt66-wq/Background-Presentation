CRITICAL BOTTLENECK

The Problem: US Passenger Train Lines

CRISIS 01
📉 Structural Deficit

Expenses Exceed Revenue

The network operates with widening operational deficits, severely limiting reinvestment in track maintenance, electrification, and passenger comfort.

Source: Goldman (2026)
⏱️ Punctuality Breakdown

Chronic Delays for Passengers

  • Fails Standards: Long-distance trains consistently fail the 80% on-time standard set by the Federal Railroad Administration.
  • Illegal Freight Priority: Freight trains frequently take priority over Amtrak despite federal law explicitly prohibiting it.
  • 1.5 Years Wasted: Freight dispatching caused over 850,000 minutes of passenger delay in a single year.
Sources: Delayed by Freight (2025) & Amtrak Host Report Card (2024)
HISTORICAL CONTEXT

History of the Problem

TIMELINE 02
A
1950s Era

Post War Pivot

  • • Post-WW2 federal policy heavily subsidized the Interstate Highway System and commercial aviation.
  • • Privately owned passenger rail could not compete with state-funded alternatives and plunged into severe financial loss.
B
1971 Creation

The Birth of Amtrak®

  • • Created by the federal government to save passenger rail from complete nationwide collapse.
  • • However, it was inaugurated with chronic underfunding, deferred maintenance, and an contradictory quasi-commercial mandate.
C
Structural Trap

Freight Ownership

  • • Unlike Europe or Asia, 97% of the US rail network is privately owned by freight companies.
  • • Amtrak must rent these tracks, stranding passenger trains behind slower, miles-long cargo convoys.

ACTIONABLE BLUEPRINT

Solutions: Initial Thoughts

REFORM 04
🛤️

Dedicated Passenger Corridors

Establish dedicated passenger rail lines adjacent to existing freight infrastructure to eliminate traffic conflicts and scheduling delays.

⚡

Infrastructure Rehabilitation

Modernize legacy tracks and signaling systems to support higher operating speeds and improve structural safety.

🌉

Grade Separation

Implement elevated or underground crossings at critical intersections to alleviate highway congestion caused by extended freight trains.
